# Support and maintenance terms

**Draft.** Read it as a supplier before you send it as one: every promise below
is one you personally have to keep, at 9pm, from wherever you are. Cut anything
you would resent.

Applies to customers holding current **annual maintenance**. A licence without
maintenance entitles the customer to the software, not to this.

---

## What is covered

| | Covered | Not covered |
|---|---|---|
| **Defects** | The module does not do what its documentation says | The module does not do something it never claimed |
| **Odoo updates** | Compatibility with point releases of the Odoo series you bought (19.0) | A new major series (20.0) — that is an upgrade, quoted separately |
| **Questions** | How to configure what you bought | Training your staff to use Odoo |
| **Data** | Advice on a problem our module caused | Recovering data lost to a backup you did not take |
| **Changes** | — | New features, custom fields, reports. Quoted as work |

**Anything a customer's own developer changed is not covered.** These modules
ship as readable Python and a customer may edit them; if they do, support
resumes at the point they restore the shipped version.

---

## Response times

Business hours are **Saturday to Thursday, 08:00–16:00 Kabul time (UTC+4:30)**,
excluding Afghan public holidays.

| Severity | Means | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Stopped** | Nobody can work. Payroll cannot run, the system will not start | 4 business hours | 2 business days, or a documented workaround |
| **2 — Impaired** | A feature is unusable but the system runs | 1 business day | 10 business days |
| **3 — Minor** | Cosmetic, or a question | 3 business days | Next release |

**These are response times, not repair times.** Severity 1 says somebody is
looking at it within four hours; it does not promise a fix within four hours,
because some fixes are not possible in four hours and promising one is how you
end up lying to a customer at midnight.

Severity is set by the supplier after reading the report. A customer who
disagrees can say so and it will be reconsidered once.

---

## How to raise something

By email, to one address, with:

- what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead
- the module and its version
- your Odoo version and whether it is Community or Enterprise
- a screenshot of any error, **including the technical details section**
- whether it happens every time

A report without the error text takes three exchanges to become actionable.
That time comes out of the response window.

---

## What we need from you

- **A named contact.** One person who can answer questions about the report.
- **A test database** for anything beyond a trivial fix. Nothing is diagnosed
  by guessing against production, and nothing is fixed there first.
- **Backups.** Taking them is the customer's responsibility. No support
  obligation extends to recovering data that was never backed up.

---

## What is explicitly not promised

Said plainly, because a customer who assumes otherwise will be disappointed at
exactly the wrong moment:

- **No 24/7 cover.** Outside business hours you may get an answer; you are not
  entitled to one.
- **No on-site attendance** unless separately agreed and paid.
- **No guaranteed uptime.** The software runs on the customer's infrastructure.
- **No support for the Odoo core**, the database, the server, or the network.
  If the problem is not in a module we sold, we will say so and stop.
- **No indefinite version support.** Each Odoo series is supported while it is
  the current one and for **twelve months** after its successor ships.

---

## Maintenance, renewal and lapse

- Maintenance runs **twelve months** from purchase and renews annually.
- **20% of the licence price**, invoiced before the renewal date.
- If it lapses, the licence keeps working: the software is bought, not rented.
  Support and updates stop, and the licence key's expiry is what the software
  itself enforces — a lapsed key gives **thirty days' grace** with a warning on
  screen, then refuses new work while continuing to show everything already
  recorded.
- Reinstating lapsed maintenance costs the missed period, so that letting it
  lapse is not cheaper than keeping it.

---

## Limits

Total liability under this agreement is limited to the licence fees paid in the
preceding twelve months. Nothing in it makes the supplier liable for lost
profits, lost data, or consequential loss.

Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

> **Have this reviewed by a lawyer in your jurisdiction before you send it to a
> customer.** It is written to be fair and readable, which is not the same as
> being enforceable where you are selling.
