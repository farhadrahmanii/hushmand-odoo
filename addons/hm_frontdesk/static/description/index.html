Front Desk

A visitor register that answers who is in the building right now — the question you need answered during a drill or an incident, not afterwards.

Odoo's Frontdesk is Enterprise. So a Community user has nowhere to record who came in, and most offices fall back to a paper book at the desk — which answers “who is here now?” only by reading every page.

At the desk

  • Expected visitors registered in advance — and checked in with one click when they arrive
  • The host notified — the moment their visitor reaches reception
  • Badge, vehicle and identification — recorded where a security desk needs them
  • How many came in together — because the register has to account for the two people who arrived with the person who was expected
  • Where they went — floor, room or department, so the desk knows

Visitors who are never checked out

That is a paper book's usual failure, and the reason a register slowly stops being trusted. A daily job finds anyone still on site from a previous day and asks their host to confirm when they left.

It does not close them automatically. Inventing a departure time nobody observed would make the register look tidy and be wrong — which is worse than a record that admits it is incomplete, because the tidy version is the one somebody would rely on.

The list you open in an emergency

On Site Now: who is in the building, with their host and where they went. One screen, no reading back through a book, and it says plainly when nobody is in the building rather than showing an empty table.
